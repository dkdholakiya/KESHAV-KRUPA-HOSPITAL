COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our hospital services, appointments, insurance, and visiting guidelines. We ensure complete transparency for your peace of mind.

Still have questions? +91 93137 51534
01

What are your visiting hours?

Our general visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM. However, for the ICU, visiting is restricted to immediate family members for short durations to ensure patient safety.

02

Do you provide 24/7 emergency services?

Yes, our emergency department and trauma center are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, staffed with experienced physicians and critical care specialists.

03

How can I book an appointment?

You can book an appointment by clicking the "Book Appointment" button on our website, calling our reception at +91 93137 51534, or visiting the hospital in person.

04

Do you accept cashless insurance?

Yes, we are empanelled with most major health insurance providers and offer cashless treatment facilities. Please bring your insurance card and a valid ID at the time of admission.

05

Do you have in-house pharmacy and diagnostic services?

Yes, Keshavkrupa Hospital features a fully stocked 24/7 pharmacy and a comprehensive pathology lab for prompt and accurate diagnostics.

06

Can I request a specific doctor for my treatment?

Absolutely. While booking your appointment, you can specify your preferred physician or specialist, and we will do our best to accommodate your request based on their availability.
